# Five companies are always collected

Apple, Microsoft, Costco, Tesla and NVIDIA are collected without being asked for, as soon as the SEC's list of companies is fetched, and again whenever one of them is found not collected. The home page draws only their revenue. We chose this because a chart of every collected company grows with each one added and stops reading as a picture, and because a fixed five means a new person sees a full home page right after setup.

This narrows [Collect companies on demand, with no fixed list](0002-collect-companies-on-demand.md): every other company is still collected only when asked for and agreed to. Setting the SEC contact is taken as agreeing to these five.

## Consequences

- Removing one of the five brings it back the next time the home page loads.
- Until they are ready, the home chart shows only the ones that are.
