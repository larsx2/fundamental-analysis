# The first version covers annual report (10-K) filers outside finance and property

The first version collects only companies that file an annual report (10-K), and it declines banks, insurers and property trusts with a reason instead of collecting them. We chose this because the glossary and the money flow view assume a company with revenue, cost of revenue and gross profit, which those businesses do not have, and because companies that file a different yearly form often follow different accounting rules, which would double the work of turning filing data into glossary terms.

A declined company is answered as not covered, before any collecting starts.

A company that renamed itself is shown as one continuous history, because the SEC keeps its number. A company that reorganised into a new legal company, as Google did into Alphabet, appears as two companies with a note linking them.

Explored in [Which companies are covered, and how they get in](../rfc/0005-which-companies.md).
